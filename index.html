About Us

Shahein Advanced Solutions Inc. (SAS Inc.) is a U.S.-based professional services and solutions firm supporting federal, state, local, and commercial organizations. We specialize in consulting, logistics, procurement, program management, and technical services designed to improve mission performance, operational efficiency, and compliance.

We operate nationwide across all 50 U.S. states and internationally, delivering high-quality, compliant, and responsive services aligned with federal acquisition regulations and industry best practices.

Our leadership team brings deep experience across government contracting, logistics operations, compliance, and business advisory services, allowing us to support complex programs from planning through execution.

Leadership Excellence

Our executive team combines decades of experience in federal contracting, homeland security operations, international logistics, and compliance management. This expertise enables us to navigate complex regulatory environments while delivering exceptional results for mission-critical operations.
